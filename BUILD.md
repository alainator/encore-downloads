# Build your own Encore setup, for any budget

Encore turns on what your hardware can do and says what it can't (Station → System → **What this setup can do**), so
you can start with what you have and add parts later. Prices are rough, in US dollars, for new parts; used ones are
cheaper. See [What your devices can do](DEVICES.md) for the details behind each row.

## 1. What you already have (about $0)

- **Host:** an Android tablet or phone with **Encore Station**, or a Windows laptop with Encore.
- **TV:** a TV with Android TV / Google TV and the **Encore TV** app, or cast to a TV with Chromecast built in.
- **Mics:** the all-in-one Bluetooth karaoke mics people already own (each sings through its own speaker).
- **You get:** the whole party: phones as remotes, the queue, the lyrics on the TV, games, profiles, covers.
- **You miss:** the singing score from a mic (it needs a mic the TV page can use), voices through the TV's speakers,
  the party's own Wi-Fi.

## 2. Starter (about $30-80 more)

- Add a **streaming stick** with Android TV / Google TV or a Fire TV stick for any TV, and install **Encore TV** on it.
- **One mic with scoring:** let the tablet draw the TV picture (screen mirroring) and sing into the tablet's mic or one
  Bluetooth mic (run the Timing check once: Bluetooth mics hear late).
- **You miss:** several scored mics, voices through the speakers without delay, the party's own Wi-Fi.

## 3. Mid (about $200-350)

- A **mini PC** (Intel N100 or N150, 16 GB) on the TV's HDMI, running Encore (Windows installer, or Linux with Docker),
  the TV page full screen on the TV.
- A **USB wireless mic set** (the receiver plugs into the mini PC): scored, and through the TV's speakers.
- Phones join over your Wi-Fi; the tablet or a laptop can be the Station.
- **You miss:** several mics scored separately (coming), the party's own Wi-Fi, a touchscreen at the box.

## 4. The ideal kit (about $500-800)

| Part | What to look for |
|---|---|
| Mini PC | Intel N100 or N150, 16 GB RAM, HDMI **and** USB-C video out, 2 or more USB ports |
| Touchscreen | USB-C portable touchscreen, about 15", one cable for picture, touch and power |
| Drive | A large external drive (8 TB holds thousands of songs) |
| Wi-Fi adapter | A USB Wi-Fi adapter that can run an access point on Linux (MediaTek MT7921AU or MT7612U chips) |
| Mics | **2-4 USB wireless mic sets**, one USB receiver per mic (or UHF mics into a multi-channel USB audio interface) |
| Speakers | The TV's, or powered speakers on the mini PC |
| Case | Optional: a carry case with room for the drive and mics |

- **You get:** everything, anywhere: its own Wi-Fi and join codes (no internet needed), the Station at the box, songs on
  its drive (synced at home from Karaoke Studio, or by USB stick), every voice through the speakers.
- **Coming:** every mic scored separately (each singer their own score), with the voices mixed by the kit itself.
- The kit software installs on Debian Linux; a step-by-step guide comes with the first kit release.
